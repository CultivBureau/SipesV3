## 2. Who SIPES is

**SIPES** is a paint and coatings brand/business serving homeowners, painters, craftspeople, architects, interior designers, contractors and construction projects. Its activities include **interior architectural paint, exterior/facade coatings, decorative/special-effects finishes, wood-care systems, tinting/color solutions, and related surface-preparation materials**. It presents itself as a producer and exporter with research-and-development capabilities and a broad distribution footprint in Egypt and the MENA region.

### Parent-company relationship

SIPES sits within **Universal Building Materials & Chemicals (UBMC Group)**, whose wider businesses address paints, decorative coatings, specialized coatings and construction chemicals. The group says it has six brands and three manufacturing facilities in Egypt. **Do not conflate other UBMC brands' products or technical capabilities with SIPES-branded products.**

The UBMC corporate history identifies the SIPES trademark in **1947**, first MENA manufacturing in **1956**, first Egyptian manufacturing in **1989**, expansion to Saudi Arabia/other markets from **2007**, PerBuild in **2009**, Transocean in **2011**, and SiTECH in **2022**. These are group-reported milestones and should not be misrepresented as the incorporation year of a particular Egyptian legal entity.

### Strategy, mission and vision (paraphrased)

- **Strategy:** Track customer needs and adapt premium products to practical requirements.
- **Vision:** A significant international paint/coatings market presence, with attention to ecological balance and product quality.
- **Mission:** Use technology, efficient operations and R&D to develop coatings that address customer needs.
- **Positioning:** Trusted decorative and protective solutions, combining choice, research, application expertise and distribution.

### Customers and use cases

- **Homeowners:** Room painting, finishing, maintenance, color matching and decorative effects.
- **Professional painters/decorators:** Putty, primer, emulsion, enamel, textured coatings and clear protective coats.
- **Architects and designers:** Broad palette, sheen choices, visual effect finishes, room previews and color centres.
- **Contractors and developers:** Building finishes, facade durability, specification support and project-scale coating solutions.
- **Wood workshops and furniture businesses:** Nitrocellulose, polyester, polyurethane, stains and varnishes.

## 9. Customer FAQ and response knowledge

**Q: How do I find the price of a SIPES product?**  
A: SIPES directs customers to branches/showrooms to obtain current product prices; no verified live online price list is available here. [FAQ](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/faq/) · [Store locator](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/store-locator/).

**Q: How can I select the right paint?**  
A: Start with substrate (concrete, gypsum, wood, metal), interior/exterior exposure, desired sheen, washability, finish/color and preparation condition. Use the relevant product page/TDS and consult the nearest color center or SIPES team if uncertain.

**Q: Are all SIPES paints washable?**  
A: No universal statement. SIPES says many types are washable, depending on product; clean gently with a damp sponge and avoid aggressive scraping. Verify the specific paint's wet-scrub resistance.

**Q: How do I calculate required liters?**  
A: Use the site's [two-coat calculator](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/calculator/) with width, height, wall type, product and windows/doors. Results are planning estimates, not guaranteed quantities.

**Q: What color can I order?**  
A: Refer to the [color catalogue](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/color-catalogue/) or Si-PRO options; color availability is product- and tinting-base-dependent. Check a physical sample since screen colors vary.

**Q: How long does SIPES paint take to dry?**  
A: No universal duration. It depends on the product, coating thickness, temperature, humidity, ventilation and recoat conditions. Consult that product's latest TDS.

**Q: Can paint be applied over wallpaper?**  
A: SIPES generally advises against painting over wallpaper. On nonporous paper, it suggests testing a small primed section first and proceeding only if no lifting/blistering occurs; evaluate the full substrate before proceeding.

**Q: Are exterior SIPES paints heat insulators?**  
A: Not by default. SIPES says exterior paints withstand weather but are **not classified as heat insulation**; customers seeking thermal insulation are directed to **PerBuild** solutions.

**Q: Where are TDS files?**  
A: Usually accessible through the individual product-detail page's technical-data-sheet link. In case of conflict or an inaccessible link, request the latest validated sheet from SIPES directly.

**Q: How can I become a distributor/franchise partner?**  
A: Submit the franchise request on the [Store Locator](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/store-locator/) or contact the company. Do not promise automatic approval or exclusive territory.

**Q: How do I find a branch?**  
A: Use the official [Store Locator](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/store-locator/), which can search and provide directions.

**Q: How can I apply for a job?**  
A: The FAQ lists **hrdept@sipes.net** for CV submissions and the site also provides careers links; verify current openings separately.

**Q: How do SIPES Points work?**  
A: Eligible product purchases include scratchable QR codes that can be scanned in the official app; points are redeemed according to app terms.

## 10. Practical workflows for product selection

### Interior wall repaint

1. Identify gypsum, cement or concrete substrate and inspect peeling, moisture, chalking and cracks.
2. Select suitable filling/preparation materials such as Latex Putty, Easy Filler, Flexi Putty or Hi-Krax if applicable.
3. Confirm a compatible sealer/primer on the product TDS (Latex Sealer 1800 is one documented example).
4. Choose final finish: e.g. **Si-Tone 700 / S7 / S70** (matt), **Si-Tone 800** (eggshell), **Si-Tone Silk** (silky), or **Bond 2000** (semi-gloss).
5. Check tinting, TDS coverage/coat count and calculate approximate quantities.

### Facade or exposed exterior

- Prioritize exterior-rated coating systems, substrate movement, UV/weather resistance and site exposure.
- **Si-Shield Matt** is a directly listed exterior product. **Si Stone**, **Si-Tex** and some other products say exterior suitability in the body but have different category placement; verify their current technical scope before use.
- Do **not** present ordinary exterior paint as thermal insulation.

### Decorative feature wall

- For metallic sparkle: consider **Acry Gold / Acry Silver** or relevant decorative metallic alkyd finishes.
- For texture: consider **Si Stone / Si-Tex / Effecto Stucco**.
- For soft decorative effects: consider **Effecto Mohair / Effecto Elizir / Effecto Amranti / Effecto Orgento**.
- Follow each decorative product's undercoat, application-tool and drying sequence; examples are **not** interchangeable.

### Wood furniture and flooring

- Stains color wood; sealers fill and seal; primers/surfacers build a base; varnishes/topcoats provide the visible finish.
- Select within a compatible chemistry: **nitrocellulose**, **polyester**, **2K polyurethane**, or specialty interior varnish.
- Do not casually substitute a nitro sealer, polyester surfacer and PU topcoat without confirmed compatibility and mixing guidance.
- For parquet, **Si-Wood 223** explicitly mentions flooring use; verify job conditions and current TDS.

## 11. Quality-control findings and knowledge gaps

1. **Category coverage ≠ complete live inventory.** Fifty individual products were verified from the main English category pages. The older catalogues and project gallery reveal other products and generations.
2. **Duracril 7000:** title says `Matt`; page body says `gloss finish`. Finish cannot be unambiguously established from that page alone.
3. **Uni-Mix:** page's leading feature bullets resemble emulsion paint, but the later description clearly identifies universal coloring concentrates.
4. **Si Stone:** categorized as Interiors/Special Effects but described for exterior use.
5. **Si-Tex and select other Interior-listed items:** body claims interior and outdoor use; product suitability must follow TDS rather than menu placement.
6. **Site publishing and spelling issues:** `Si-Tone 710` uses an unrelated URL slug; `SUIZ`, `SAISHIELD`, `Elisir`/`Elizir`, and `Si Tone Silk 700` appear in copy. Store canonical labels and aliases separately.
7. **Coverage and units:** some pages list `m²/kg`, others `m²/L`, and at least one gives `6–8 square meters` without an explicit denominator. Do not convert or infer units without verified density/conditions.
8. **Product claims:** conformity with Egyptian standards is claimed on certain product pages; current certificate status, test reports, ISO scope and regulatory status are **not independently verified**.
9. **Technical safety:** Many products are solvent-borne or 2K chemically cured. Read current labels, SDS, TDS and relevant safe-handling instructions. Do not supply universal safety, pot-life, VOC, mixing or fire-risk advice across products.
10. **Catalogues are date-specific:** 2017/2019/2021/2023 references may not describe products available in November 2026.
11. **Pricing, stock and geography:** current prices, inventory, packaging and channel availability require direct company/distributor confirmation.
12. **Company vs group:** UBMC's marine, industrial, construction-chemical and other-branded offerings must not be assigned to the SIPES brand by default.
13. **Dynamic content:** The interactive distributor map, color combinations and some PDFs are not reproduced exhaustively in this text KB.

## 12. Recommended usage as an AI-agent or RAG knowledge base

**Answering principles:**

- Identify the intended surface, indoor/outdoor exposure, preferred aesthetic/finish, desired performance and project location before recommending a product.
- Recommend only when a SIPES page clearly matches the request; cite its exact URL.
- When specs differ or are missing, say so and route to a current TDS or SIPES technical support instead of fabricating facts.
- Distinguish generic educational guidance from confirmed SIPES product claims.
- Never give a definite price, stock status, paint coverage, curing time, mixing ratio, flame rating, certification or warranty without an applicable current source.
- Avoid treating old PDF catalogue content as current or treating group-brand product claims as SIPES claims.
- For construction-chemistry/safety-critical decisions, advise consulting qualified technical support and the current SDS/TDS.

**Suggested searchable metadata per product:** `product_name`, `aliases`, `category`, `chemistry`, `finish`, `application_indoor_outdoor`, `substrates`, `strengths`, `limitations`, `mixing_ratio`, `dry_time`, `source_url`, `tds_url`, `verified_on`, `source_conflict_flag`.

**Example safe product answer:** “For a washable matt interior wall finish, S70 and Si-Tone 700 are listed as water-based matt coatings. The S70 page explicitly mentions wet-scrub resistance, while Si-Tone 700 cites hiding and alkali resistance. Actual cleaning performance and compatibility must be checked in the current TDS for your selected color and substrate.”
