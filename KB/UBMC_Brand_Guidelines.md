# UBMC Group — Brand Guidelines

## Important status and usage note

**This document is an evidence-based *working* brand guide, not a verified official UBMC brand-book PDF.** It combines: (1) positioning, portfolio and messaging that UBMC publishes on its websites, (2) visible aesthetic cues from public UBMC identity material, and (3) **clearly marked proposed design rules** for consistent new content.

**Not verified as official:** Exact logo master files, Pantone/CMYK/RGB/HEX values, licensed typeface, grid system, minimum clear space, photography standards and legally approved copy. Obtain the logo asset and formal specification from UBMC before producing final approved brand materials. Do not present the suggested color codes or typography choices below as official UBMC specifications.

**Companion corporate knowledge base:** [UBMC_Company_Knowledge_Base.md](UBMC_Company_Knowledge_Base.md).

| UBMC Color Center | Company-operated stores and advice | [Open Color Center](https://ubmcgroup.com/ubmc-color-center/) |

**Recommended hierarchy for new materials:**

1. **Corporate assets** (company profile, group website, recruitment, manufacturing facts): Lead with UBMC.
2. **Product assets** (product pages, application instructions, packaging mockups): Lead with the relevant product brand and use UBMC as a parent endorsement only where permission and original brand assets support it.
3. **Multi-brand assets** (showroom campaigns, exhibitions): Lead with UBMC, then display individual brand logos with equal visual care.
4. **International Transocean assets:** Do not imply all international Transocean member companies are owned by UBMC. Its global network has a separate published identity.

**Never** merge or recolor subsidiary-brand marks into a made-up common lockup without approval. Their individual identities may use different graphics, colors and fonts.

## 03. Logo treatment

### Observed identity cue

UBMC publicly uses a group wordmark **“UBMC”** with an adjacent graphic emblem and the full company descriptor. A publicly indexed photo of UBMC/SIPES office signage shows this lockup in a **dark burgundy/magenta-red family** on a light ground. The sign is only a visual reference; it is **not** a color-calibrated source.

### Recommended rules — pending official master files

- Use the **original vector logo** (SVG, AI or EPS) from UBMC whenever available.
- Preserve the logo's proportions; never stretch, rotate, redraw, substitute the emblem or retype the wordmark.
- Use the original approved full-color version on white or clean light backgrounds.
- For dark backgrounds, use an **approved reverse/white version**, not an automatically inverted logo.
- Keep conspicuous whitespace around the whole mark. **Provisional clear space:** at least the height of the capital “U” on every side, until official spacing is available.
- **Provisional minimum size:** Ensure the complete descriptor is actually legible. Where too small, use a legitimately approved compact logo, not an improvised crop.
- Do not put product photos, gradients, shadows or fine patterns behind the logo that reduce contrast.
- Do not distort the emblem to create new icons or logos.

**Asset source:** [Official UBMC homepage](https://ubmcgroup.com/). Request a vector export from the company before final print or public release.

| `surface-warm` | `#F7F5F3` | Alternating light sections | **Proposed neutral** |
| `stroke-light` | `#E4E0DF` | Hairline borders/dividers | **Proposed neutral** |

**How to use the provisional palette:**
- Prefer white/light backgrounds and restrained use of the main accent; products and application photography should remain the focus.
- Use brand-primary for one major focal point per layout, not for every text line.
- Keep body text dark on light surfaces, with sufficient accessibility contrast.
- Avoid claiming these HEX values match printed signs, product packaging or UBMC's official color values.
- For print, ask UBMC for the official **Pantone/CMYK values**. Do **not** auto-convert this approximation and label it “brand accurate.”

## 05. Typography

**Status: RECOMMENDED substitutes; official UBMC fonts were not authenticated.**

| Language/use | Working choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| English headlines and interface | **Inter** or **Montserrat** | Clear modern sans-serif hierarchy |
| English body | **Inter** | High readability in digital content |
| Arabic headings/body | **Cairo** or **Tajawal** | Legible contemporary Arabic suitable for bilingual layouts |
| Numeric data and specification sheets | Inter with tabular numerals, where available | Consistent numerical alignment |

### Suggested editorial hierarchy

- **Hero H1:** 40–56 px desktop / 30–38 px mobile, strong weight.
- **Section H2:** 28–36 px desktop / 24–28 px mobile.
- **Cards/H3:** 18–24 px.
- **Body:** 16–18 px; line height ~1.5–1.7.
- **Metadata/captions:** 12–14 px.
- Keep text alignment left for English, right for Arabic, and respect correct right-to-left direction for Arabic interfaces.
- Use sentence case for explanatory copy; reserve all caps for small labels where appropriate.

These are implementation recommendations, not officially measured dimensions.

## 06. Brand voice and tone

### Grounded brand voice attributes

**Professional**, **reassuring through competence**, **innovative**, **quality-focused**, **customer-oriented**, and **solution-led**.

### Recommended writing style

- Lead with the customer's application or challenge, then explain the relevant solution.
- Use specific product categories and performance requirements rather than unsupported promises.
- When writing for specialists, name surfaces, conditions, product type and technical limitations accurately.
- For a general audience, avoid acronyms without explanation and link to the relevant brand's page.
- Avoid saying a product is “the best,” “guaranteed for any condition,” “100% green” or “certified” without product-specific evidence.
- Do not claim a particular product is available, approved or safe for an application without a current technical data sheet and brand confirmation.

### Example copy — newly written, not an official slogan

**Group-level website intro:**
> UBMC brings together decorative paints, professional coatings, marine protection and construction chemicals to support projects from surface preparation to lasting finish.

**Quality-focused statement:**
> Discover specialized solutions supported by manufacturing expertise, technical development and a commitment to quality.

**Store CTA:**
> Explore our brands and speak with a specialist at a UBMC Color Center.

**Brand navigation CTA:**
> Looking for an application-specific solution? Explore SIPES, SiTech, Transocean Coatings, PerBuild or Swift Paints.

## 08. Photography and visual direction — proposed

**Preferred subjects**

- Finished interiors, residential and commercial façades with visible surface quality.
- Concrete and construction environments, professional application and workmanship.
- Industrial assets, marine environments and coated infrastructure for specialist business areas.
- Laboratory/manufacturing/R&D environments when discussing expertise and quality.
- Products and packaging shown accurately and prominently when the product brand is the focus.

**Art direction**

- Bright, realistic, high-resolution photography with clear surfaces and material detail.
- Balanced lighting, realistic colors and credible professional context.
- Show people using appropriate occupational protective equipment in technical settings.
- Favor authentic Egyptian/regional architecture and contexts where relevant; avoid generic stock-style claims of UBMC locations.
- Leave uncluttered negative space for headlines and CTA placement.

**Avoid**

- False “before/after” transformations presented as evidence.
- Unsubstantiated performance diagrams or doctored test results.
- Recolored product buckets or altered safety labels.
- Confusing images of unrelated industrial products with a specific UBMC brand/SKU.

## 09. Digital and social templates — proposed

### Layout principles

- Favor a white/light canvas, a single corporate accent, clean typography and precise alignment.
- Provide clear visual hierarchy: short headline → one application/benefit → product or category → visible CTA.
- Use clean grids and generous line height; avoid clutter from too many brand colors on a single panel.
- On multi-brand pages, make each brand a separate card with **its own official logo** and a clearly labeled link.

### Suggested formats (platform-dependent; not UBMC standards)

- **Instagram feed:** 1080 × 1350 px, with legible headline and safe margins.
- **Instagram/Reels/Stories:** 1080 × 1920 px; protect content against app overlays.
- **LinkedIn square:** 1200 × 1200 px or a suitable platform-recommended ratio.
- **Website hero:** responsive images and short, legible copy; use web-optimized formats.
- **Product brochures:** full-resolution approved product photos, technical tables and links to current data sheets.

### Example carousel structure

1. Customer problem or application.
2. Why the surface/application needs the right product family.
3. Relevant UBMC brand and product category.
4. Evidence or technical point with source.
5. Clear CTA to the correct brand or contact route.

## 10. UX and accessibility — proposed

- Ensure normal body text meets **WCAG 2.2 AA contrast**, targeting at least **4.5:1** where applicable.
- Avoid text embedded in photographs when it harms readability; use a solid/gradient overlay as needed.
- Give all brand logos meaningful alt text, e.g., `SIPES — decorative paints`.
- Use descriptive links: “Explore PerBuild construction chemicals,” not just “click here.”
- Support keyboard focus, semantic headings, accessible link states and screen-reader labels.
- Present Arabic with proper RTL structure; retain Latin product codes in their original form.
- Keep product names, safety claims, technical data and certifications exact to current approved materials.

## 11. Corporate vs. sub-brand do/don't rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use **UBMC** to introduce the group and its portfolio | Call every UBMC brand an incorporated company without documentation |
| Send a visitor directly to the right brand site | Route every specialist question to a generic homepage only |
| Keep each brand's official logo and distinct voice | Force all brands into one invented color/logo system |
| Make claims traceable to published product or technical documentation | Invent certification scope, quantities, product performance or prices |
| Explain when a rule is a working design recommendation | Present approximate colors or fonts as formally approved |
| Clarify the international Transocean relationship | Imply UBMC owns the entire worldwide Transocean association |

## 13. Validation checklist before publishing

- [ ] Obtain current UBMC logo master file and any approved reversed/monochrome variants.
- [ ] Request official color specifications (HEX/RGB/CMYK/Pantone) and fonts.
- [ ] Confirm company-approved copy, corporate naming, legal attribution and group/brand relationships.
- [ ] Verify certificates, factory figures, product claims and product photography with approved sources.
- [ ] Check each brand hyperlink and mobile/desktop presentation.
- [ ] Confirm product availability, dealer details and current store hours before publishing promotions.
- [ ] Confirm trademark/usage permissions for all six brand marks, especially Transocean's global identity.
- [ ] Test accessibility, bilingual layout and responsive behavior.
