## 01. Who UBMC is

**Full name:** Universal Building Materials and Chemicals Company (UBMC). The UBMC Group is an Egypt-headquartered provider and manufacturer of **paints, decorative finishing products, professional and protective coatings, and construction chemicals**. It positions itself as a multi-brand source for integrated building, decoration, protection and construction solutions.

**Geographical focus:** Egypt, the Middle East and Africa; UBMC reports serving Egypt and **15 other countries**, with regional distribution and exports.

**Brand model:** The UBMC corporate website identifies **six brands/business units**: **SIPES, SiTech Coatings, Transocean Coatings, PerBuild, Swift Paints, and UBMC Color Center**. The first five focus on product/solution areas; UBMC Color Center is a **company-operated retail/store network**, not simply another coating manufacturer.

**In one sentence:** UBMC combines decorative paints, specialist coatings, marine/industrial protective coatings, construction chemicals and retail advice/distribution under one group brand.

Official reading: [About UBMC](https://ubmcgroup.com/about-company/), [Homepage](https://ubmcgroup.com/), [Export](https://ubmcgroup.com/about-export/).

## 03. Brands and business units — direct links

### 3.1 [SIPES — official website](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/)

**Positioning:** The decorative and architectural painting brand, serving homeowners, decorators, craftspeople, designers, architects and projects.

**Offers:**
- Interior painting systems, including emulsions and associated fillers/putties.
- Exterior painting solutions.
- Wood-care products and varnishes.
- Decorative finishes and special-effect coatings.
- Color selection, color inspiration and catalogues; UBMC describes a **Si-PRO tinting system** with **over 900 color tones**.

**Representative product examples from the SIPES catalogue:** Bond 5000, Easy Filler, S70, Si-Tone products, Latex Putty, Flexi Putty, Varno, Ultra Clear, Si Wood 223, SipeStar, and Si-Shield Matt. These are **examples, not a full or guaranteed current inventory**.

**Who to refer:** Customers asking for painting a home, wall preparation, paint colors, decorative finishes, wood finishes, interior/exterior products or SIPES catalogues.

### 3.2 [SiTech Coatings — official website](https://ubmcgroup.com/sitech/)

**Positioning:** Premium/professional high-performance coatings, particularly for demanding exterior surfaces and specialist applications.

**Offers:**
- Exterior and façade coating systems developed for weathering and demanding urban, coastal and industrial environments.
- Floor coating products such as self-leveling epoxies, mortars, grouts and crack fillers.
- Specialized interior paints, including antibacterial and cooling paint categories described on the site.
- Protection of concrete floors from exposure to oils, chemicals, abrasion and impact.

**Examples highlighted on its website:** XTR Putty, XTR Sealer S1, XTR Sealer S100 and XTR Roof.

**Who to refer:** Projects asking for specialty façades, protective floor treatments, epoxy systems, industrial or coastal exposure, and specialist architectural coatings.

**Useful deep links:** [About SiTech](https://ubmcgroup.com/sitech/about/), [SiTech site](https://ubmcgroup.com/sitech/).

### 3.3 [Transocean Coatings — UBMC brand overview](https://ubmcgroup.com/transocean-coatings/)

**Additional product/reference website:** [Transocean Coatings international site](https://transocean-coatings.com/).

**Positioning:** Marine, yacht, offshore and industrial/protective coating solutions.

**Offers:**
- **Marine coatings:** ship protection, including antifouling and corrosion protection.
- **Offshore and protective coatings:** systems for exposed steel and industrial structures.
- **Yacht coatings:** protective and decorative finishes for pleasure craft.
- Solutions associated with tank linings, pipelines, vessels, infrastructure and industrial installations.

**Who to refer:** Shipyards, marine operators, engineering contractors, offshore assets, industrial protection projects and yacht owners/maintainers.

**Important organization clarification:** The global Transocean Coatings website describes an international association established in **1959**, with its central office in the **Netherlands**. UBMC's own site says the Transocean brand was **introduced in its portfolio in 2011**. Do **not** interpret UBMC's portfolio page as ownership of every company in the global association.

**Useful deep links:** [Marine coatings](https://transocean-coatings.com/), [Product search](https://transocean-coatings.com/), [UBMC portfolio page](https://ubmcgroup.com/transocean-coatings/).

### 3.4 [PerBuild — official website](https://ubmcgroup.com/perbuild/)

**Positioning:** Construction chemicals and building-performance materials for the local and export markets.

**Offers:**
- Premixed cementitious products, mortars and repair-oriented materials.
- Concrete admixtures and mortar admixtures.
- Waterproofing products and systems.
- Grouts, tile adhesives and other chemical/specialty construction applications described in the UBMC overview.
- Flooring epoxies and surface-protection solutions noted in its corporate brand description.

**Services:** PerBuild specifically advertises **samples/trial sessions**, theoretical and practical technical support, **technical supervision**, application guidance and product recommendations.

**Who to refer:** Civil contractors, site engineers, concrete specialists, developers, waterproofing contractors and building-material specifiers.

**Useful deep links:**
- [About PerBuild](https://ubmcgroup.com/perbuild/aboutus/)
- [PerBuild product categories](https://ubmcgroup.com/perbuild/products/)

**Catalogue caveat:** The UBMC portfolio page refers to *more than 30 products*, while PerBuild's own website mentions *more than 60*. Because these figures are inconsistent, refer users to its live product catalogue instead of quoting an exact total.

## 05. What UBMC delivers beyond products

### Product development and manufacturing

UBMC operates manufacturing assets for paint, coatings and chemical solutions. Its public materials emphasize controlled production, raw-material selection and quality checks.

### Research & development

The Group identifies a dedicated R&D function and trained technical teams as the basis for new formulations and products.

### Quality assurance, safety and certifications

UBMC describes internal quality procedures, industrial safety standards, and management-system recognition. **Certification scope and validity should be verified from current certificates** before citing a particular ISO certificate for an individual product, factory or brand.

### Distribution and exports

UBMC supplies local customers through distribution, facilities and warehouses, and reports exporting to markets in the Middle East and Africa. Its website states service in Egypt **plus 15 other countries**.

### Technical advice and project support

- PerBuild explicitly advertises product recommendations, theoretical/practical sessions and technical supervision.
- UBMC Color Center advertises specialist customer advice.
- For specialized technical specifications, customers should contact the relevant brand for product data sheets and installation/application conditions.

### Project participation

UBMC references private/national project experience and showcases selected building developments on its corporate project page.

## 10. Suggested knowledge-base Q&A

**Q: What does UBMC stand for?**  
A: Universal Building Materials and Chemicals Company.

**Q: Is UBMC only a paint company?**  
A: No. Its portfolio also spans protective and marine coatings, construction chemicals, specialist architectural coatings, and stores/product advice.

**Q: Which UBMC company makes decorative paints?**  
A: [SIPES](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/), which covers architectural/decorative paints and finishes.

**Q: Which brand handles floor and specialized performance coatings?**  
A: [SiTech Coatings](https://ubmcgroup.com/sitech/).

**Q: Which brand serves marine and industrial corrosion protection?**  
A: [Transocean Coatings](https://ubmcgroup.com/transocean-coatings/). For global product information, see [Transocean's international website](https://transocean-coatings.com/).

**Q: Which brand handles concrete admixtures and waterproofing?**  
A: [PerBuild](https://ubmcgroup.com/perbuild/).

**Q: Which brand provides cost-conscious painting products?**  
A: [Swift Paints](https://ubmcgroup.com/swift-paints/).

**Q: Where can I visit a physical store?**  
A: [UBMC Color Center](https://ubmcgroup.com/ubmc-color-center/) lists stores in Heliopolis and several Giza locations.

**Q: Can UBMC provide technical application guidance?**  
A: PerBuild explicitly describes technical advice, trial sessions and supervision; use the [PerBuild site](https://ubmcgroup.com/perbuild/) or [group contact page](https://ubmcgroup.com/contact-us/) for routing.

**Q: How can I get current prices or stock availability?**  
A: The company websites do not establish one universal real-time price/stock inventory. Contact the relevant brand or an official Color Center; SIPES also directs customers to branches for price lists in its [FAQ](https://ubmcgroup.com/sipes/en/faq/).

**Q: Does UBMC own every company on the worldwide Transocean website?**  
A: The available public sources **do not support that conclusion**. The worldwide Transocean site describes a separate international member network; UBMC lists Transocean as part of its brand offering.

## 12. Accuracy and maintenance notes

1. **Ownership terms:** Publicly described as “brands under UBMC's umbrella”; do not automatically convert these into separately registered subsidiaries or assert specific legal structures.
2. **Prices & availability:** Prices, stock, distributors and live store hours require direct confirmation.
3. **Catalog completeness:** This file describes category coverage and examples, not a complete product-by-product scrape.
4. **Claims and figures:** Recheck manufacturing numbers, reach, employee count and certification status before investor, legal or regulatory use.
5. **Conflicting catalog counts:** PerBuild public pages report different product counts; do not promise a numerical total.
6. **Website placeholders:** Some homepage counters return zero in parsed text despite other pages giving figures. Do not present `0` as real operational data.
7. **Maintenance:** Suggested review interval is quarterly and whenever UBMC updates a brand website or launches a new product category.
